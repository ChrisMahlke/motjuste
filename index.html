02 // About Mot Juste

The exactly right word, kept until it's yours.

Mot juste is French, used in English, for the exactly right word. Mot Juste helps you find it, then keeps it until it's yours.

A dictionary of more than 125,000 words from Open English WordNet and English Wiktionary, with 837 entries written for the app, each with notes, examples, and its origin, and 288 groups of close words told apart.

What it does:

  1. 01Looks up any of more than 125,000 words, even offline
  2. 02Names the word you can't think of from what you mean, or finds a kinder, harsher, more formal, or more casual one
  3. 03Shows the close words you might weigh a word against, how each differs, and a sentence where each fits best
  4. 04Sharpens a paragraph: marks everyday words, padding, clichés, and repeats, says why, and offers better words, in the app, the share sheet, or with Siri
  5. 05Saves words from Safari, Books, Kindle, or a printed page, and keeps the sentence you found each one in, in your Commonplace Book
  6. 06Brings three new words a day, chosen for what you write, in a morning notification and in widgets on the Home Screen, the Lock Screen, and StandBy
  7. 07Brings each word back in a short review just before you'd forget it, until you can choose it over its close words
  8. 08Keeps the sentences you write with your words, sets a short writing prompt each week, and shows the words you lean on
  9. 09Quizzes meanings, gaps, opposites, and spelling, with forty lessons on punctuation, grammar, and style
  10. 10With Apple Intelligence, checks a sentence you write, and writes a short story from the words you're learning
  11. 11Keeps your words, lists, reviews, and Commonplace Book the same on iPhone and iPad through iCloud

Your progress:

The words you've used, the close words you can tell apart, and every review of the last 30 days.

Works offline · no account
