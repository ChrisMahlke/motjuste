Support

Questions, problems, and ideas

To report a problem or suggest something, contact M10 LLC through m10sys.ai.studio ↗. I read everything.

It helps to include your iPhone or iPad, its iOS version, the word or screen you were on, and what you expected to see.

Requirements

  • iPhone or iPad with iOS 17 or later
  • For checking sentences in Use It, Stories, and Find the Word's suggestions: a device that supports Apple Intelligence, on iOS 26 or later, with Apple Intelligence turned on
  • iCloud (optional), to keep your words, lists, reviews, and Commonplace Book the same on iPhone and iPad
  • No internet connection: the dictionary, Find the Word, lessons, reviews, and quizzes are all on your device

Common questions

Where are Stories, and why doesn't Use It check my sentence? Both need Apple Intelligence: a device that supports it, on iOS 26 or later, with Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings. Without it, Stories is hidden and Use It keeps your sentence on the word's page without checking it. Everything else works. Find the Word still lists the dictionary's matches, without Apple Intelligence's suggestions.

How do I save a word I've found somewhere else? In Safari, select the word, then tap Share, then Mot Juste. You'll see its meaning, and Save adds it with the sentence it came from. Highlights in Books and Kindle work the same way. The sentence also goes to your Commonplace Book, with the page it came from. If Mot Juste isn't in the share sheet, look under Edit Actions.

Can Mot Juste help with text in another app? Select a sentence or a paragraph, tap Share, then Mot Juste. Sharpen marks its everyday words, padding, and clichés and offers better words; Find a Word finds the word for what it describes; Save keeps the text on Home for later, where you can tap any word in it.

Can I look up a word on a printed page? Tap the + button at the top of Home, then Scan a Page, and hold the page up to the camera. Text from a Photo does the same for a photo you've already taken, and Paste Text for anything you've copied. Tap any word to look it up.

Can I bring in the words I looked up on my Kindle? Yes. Tap the + button at the top of Home, then Import from Kindle or Highlights, and choose Kindle's Vocabulary Builder file (vocab.db), its My Clippings.txt, or a CSV of highlights from another app. You choose which words to keep, and each one's sentence and book go to your Commonplace Book.

How do I find a word I can't think of? Open Find the Word, on Home or in Search. Describe what you mean ("a fear of open spaces" finds agoraphobia, and "a kinder word for stubborn" finds words in another tone), or Compare two close words to see how they differ.

What does Sharpen do? Write or paste a sentence or a whole paragraph. Sharpen marks everyday words ("walked", "very big"), padding ("past history"), clichés, hedges, and repeated words, says why each is worth a look, and offers better words. Tap one to put it in, Cut It to drop padding, or Leave It. It works offline, and nothing you write leaves your device.

What are Choose Between and Shift the Tone? Many words have close neighbors that mean nearly the same thing. On a word's page, Choose Between shows how each one differs and a sentence where each fits best; for some, Show Map places them on a small chart instead. Shift the Tone offers the same meaning in a word that's more formal, more casual, kinder, harsher, stronger, or milder. Tags under the part of speech say when a word is formal, informal, literary, or dated, and when it approves or disapproves.

How do I get today's words each morning? Tap Turn On under today's words on Home, or tap the gear on Home, then turn on Daily Notification under Today's Words and choose a time. Touch and hold the notification and choose Quiz Me for a quiz on the day's three words. If notifications are off for Mot Juste, allow them in the Settings app.

How do I put today's words on my Lock Screen? Add a Mot Juste widget. Word of the Day shows the first of today's words on the Home Screen, the Lock Screen, and in StandBy. Today's Words shows all three on the Home Screen and the Lock Screen.

When do today's words change? At midnight, your time. They stay the same all day, even after you save or study one, and they leave out words you've already saved or studied.

Why do today's words suit what I write? The first time you open Mot Juste, it asks what you write: everyday writing, work, study, fiction and essays, or finance. Today's words and reviews lean toward those kinds of writing. To change it, tap the gear on Home, then What You Write.

How do reviews work? Each word comes back just before you'd be likely to forget it, and the questions get harder as you learn it: first a recall card, then choose the meaning, then choose the word or fill the gap. Once you know a word well, if it has close neighbors, the last question is Which Fits Best?: choose it over the words it's easily confused with. Progress, under Your Words on Home, shows how many words you've used in your own sentences, how many close words you can tell apart, and your reviews.

How is the Word Quiz different? A quiz is practice whenever you want it. Choose the words (today's, a list, the words due for review, or the ones you've missed) and 5, 10, or 20 questions. Besides meanings, gaps, opposites, and spelling, it asks you to make a sentence more exact, pick the word that goes with another, and spot the word that strikes the wrong note. Missing a word that's in review brings it back sooner; getting one right leaves its review as it was.

What's the Commonplace Book? Every sentence you save a word from, whether from Safari, a photo, a scanned page, or a Kindle, is kept there with where it came from, along with the sentences you write in Use It and your pieces for This Week's Prompt. Open it under Your Words on Home. You can search it, group it by date, source, or word, and export it as a Markdown file.

What's This Week's Prompt? Each week, Practice on Home offers a short scene to write about with a few of your words. Your piece goes to your Commonplace Book. Challenge a Friend sends the prompt as a file; a friend who opens it in Mot Juste can write their own and send it back. You can share a list of words the same way: open the list on the Words tab, tap ⋯, then Share List.

What's Writing Habits? It counts the words and phrases you lean on in the writing you check with Sharpen, Use It, and the weekly prompt, and suggests other ways to say them. Only the counts are kept, on your device, and you can clear them.

Is there a Mot Juste keyboard? Yes. In the Settings app, go to General, then Keyboard, then Keyboards, then Add New Keyboard, and choose Mot Juste. As you type, its suggestion bar offers more exact words for the word you've just typed ("walked" becomes strolled, ambled, or strode). It doesn't need Full Access, and nothing you type leaves the keyboard.

When is a grammar lesson mastered? When its best Grammar Quiz score reaches 80%, in a quiz that asked at least three of its questions.

How do I take a word out of My Words? On the word's page, tap Remove beside In My Words, or touch and hold it in the index or in Search and choose Remove from My Words. To remove several at once, open My Words on the Words tab, tap ⋯, then Select Words to Remove. Undo puts them back, and your study progress is kept.

My words aren't on my other device. Sign in to iCloud with the same Apple Account on both, and check that iCloud is on for Mot Juste in the Settings app. Your words, lists, place in Words, reviews, and Commonplace Book sync. Quiz history, grammar progress, and writing habits stay on the device where you made them.

How do I back up my words? On Home, under Your Words, open Manage Content, then Export Backup. The file holds the words you've added and changed, and Import Backup brings them back. Study progress isn't included. To keep a copy of your Commonplace Book, export it from its ⋯ menu.

Can I use Siri? Yes. Say "Define a word in Mot Juste", "Find a word in Mot Juste", "Compare two words in Mot Juste", or "Sharpen my writing with Mot Juste", or use those actions in Shortcuts. Your words appear in Spotlight too.

How do I switch between dark and light? Tap the gear on Home, then Appearance: Automatic, which follows your device, Light, or Dark.

Does Mot Juste work offline? Yes. The first time you tap a word in a sentence, iOS may download English language data so Mot Juste can tell the word's part of speech. Until it has, words still open.

What languages is Mot Juste in? English. The app, its dictionary, and its lessons are in U.S. English, with pronunciations in General American, whatever language your device uses.

Where do the definitions come from? Mot Juste's own 837 words were written for the app, with notes, examples, and origins, and so were the close-word comparisons, tone tags, word roots, French expressions, and Words For… themes. The sentences under In Literature are from books old enough to be in the public domain. The rest of the dictionary comes from Open English WordNet and English Wiktionary, with pronunciations from the CMU Pronouncing Dictionary, in General American. The credits are in Settings, under About.

How do I say "Mot Juste"? moh·ZHOOST (/ˌmoʊ ˈʒust/). It's French, used in English, for the exactly right word, and it's one of the app's own words.
