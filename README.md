Mot Juste is an iPhone and iPad app for finding, keeping, and practising English words: an offline dictionary, three new words a day, spaced review, quizzes, and punctuation and grammar lessons. No account, no ads, no tracking.

This repository is the support channel for the app. To report a problem or suggest something, [open an issue](https://github.com/ChrisMahlke/motjuste/issues/new) with your device, its iOS version, and what you expected to see. The [support page](https://chrismahlke.github.io/motjuste/support/) answers common questions.
